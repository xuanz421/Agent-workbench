# Prose Revision

Use this skill after the story intent and scene function are understood.

## Workflow

1. Identify the intended voice, viewpoint, tense, emotional register, and scene purpose before editing.
2. Preserve canon, meaning, and characterization unless the requested revision explicitly changes them.
3. Remove redundancy, generic intensifiers, repeated emotional explanation, and filler transitions.
4. Replace vague abstraction with concrete action, image, sensory detail, or implication only where it improves precision.
5. Adjust sentence and paragraph rhythm to match tension, reflection, comedy, intimacy, or action.
6. Check dialogue for distinct voice, agenda, subtext, interruption, and believable omission.
7. Reduce explanatory narration when action, consequence, or dialogue already communicates the same point.
8. Keep deliberate repetition, stylization, ambiguity, or restraint when they serve the voice.
9. Re-read the revised passage for continuity of tone and causal flow, not just sentence-level polish.

## Guardrails

- Do not homogenize all prose into a single "literary" style.
- Do not replace simple precise language with ornate synonyms for variety alone.
- Do not remove every adverb, fragment, repetition, or passive construction mechanically.
- Do not intensify emotion by adding constant bodily reactions or metaphors.
- Do not change plot facts while performing prose-only revision unless a contradiction must be flagged.
